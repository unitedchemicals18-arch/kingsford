Our Founding Purpose

Formulating Safer, Stronger Chemical Technologies

Founded with a commitment to elevate commercial hygiene and industrial degreasing standards, Kingsford British Chemical Industries develops high-performance cleaning solutions engineered to out-clean conventional solvents without hazardous fumes or surface degradation.

Whether synthesizing our broad-spectrum Multi-Surface Disinfectant Pro for hospital care facilities or developing ultra-clinging snow foam for nationwide commercial fleets, our chemistry lab ensures maximum active-ingredient density, superior dilution economy, and strict OSHA/GHS compliance.

We partner directly with commercial contractors, food processing complexes, school districts, and manufacturing plants, eliminating middleman distribution markups while providing custom metering and application support.
